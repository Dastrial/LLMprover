LIBRARY OBJECTS

Prefer simple Rocq tactics and authorized automation when they can solve the
goal robustly.

You may use library facts from memory when useful, but prefer simple,
well-known objects over complicated or uncertain APIs.

If a library invocation fails, use the Rocq error and history to adjust the
name, arguments, or strategy rather than blindly repeating the same attempt.

Prefer a simple tactic proof over a fragile proof that depends on a complicated
guessed library interface.
