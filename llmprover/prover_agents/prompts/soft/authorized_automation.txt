AUTHORIZED AUTOMATION

The evaluator makes the following standard automation tactics available when
checking generated proof scripts:

lia
nia
lra
nra
ring
ring_simplify
field
field_simplify
auto
eauto
firstorder
congruence

You may freely use these tactics when mathematically appropriate even if their
implementation modules are absent from the original theorem header.

This permission grants use of these tactics only. It does NOT grant general
access to additional library lemmas from the modules used internally to
implement them.

Other automation tactics that require imports absent from the original theorem
header are not authorized.

Prefer authorized automation when it can robustly solve routine:
- arithmetic;
- polynomial normalization;
- field normalization;
- elementary logical proof search;
- congruence reasoning.

In particular, when appropriate:
- `lia` / `nia` for suitable integer or natural arithmetic;
- `lra` / `nra` for suitable real or rational arithmetic;
- `ring` / `ring_simplify` for polynomial normalization;
- `field` / `field_simplify` for appropriate field expressions;
- `auto` / `eauto` / `firstorder` / `congruence` for suitable routine logical
  or structural reasoning.
