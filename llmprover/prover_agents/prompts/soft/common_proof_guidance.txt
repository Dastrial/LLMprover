COMMON PROOF GUIDANCE

Prefer short, robust proofs that completely close the lemma.

Prefer simple one-command-per-line scripts when possible.
When possible, keep lines under 80 characters.

Useful basic Rocq tactics include:
`intros`, `exact`, `apply`, `rewrite`, `simpl`, `cbn`, `unfold`,
`reflexivity`, `destruct`, `induction`, `assert`, `pose proof`,
`specialize`, `compute`, `constructor`, `split`, `left`, `right`, `exists`,
`discriminate`, `inversion`, `subst`, `change`, `replace`, `symmetry`,
`transitivity`.

Consider case analysis or induction only when the structure of the theorem
actually requires it.

For closed concrete computations, prefer simple computation or appropriate
authorized automation.

If you use `simpl`, `cbn`, `compute`, `ring_simplify`, `field_simplify`, or
another normalization tactic, do not assume normalization alone finishes the
theorem — verify that all resulting goals are closed.

After `apply`, `rewrite`, `assert`, or `replace`, consider using a tactical such
as `; auto` when appropriate to discharge routine side goals immediately.
Do not rely on it blindly: make sure all remaining goals are understood.

When a remaining goal appears routine, consider a simple closing tactic such as
`reflexivity`, `assumption`, `exact`, `auto`, or suitable authorized automation.

Computation or normalization may simplify a goal without closing it; sometimes
a final `reflexivity` or another small closing step is still needed.

Use `repeat` only when repeated execution is expected to make clear progress
toward termination.

Be especially careful with repeated rewriting: a rewrite can expose another
instance of the same pattern and lead to very long or nonterminating tactic
execution.

Do not substitute Lean tactics such as `simp`, `rw`, `linarith`, `nlinarith`,
or `norm_num` for their Rocq counterparts.
Avoid Lean-style standalone `by` proof blocks and other Lean-specific proof
syntax.
Do not infer from this that the token `by` is forbidden in Rocq: use it only
where it is valid Rocq syntax, for example in `assert (H : P) by lia.`.
