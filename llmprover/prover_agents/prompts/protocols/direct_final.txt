OUTPUT PROTOCOL — FINAL DIRECT / REPAIR ANSWER

When the current turn is the FINAL ANSWER for a direct or repair proof, the
response is only the complete Rocq tactic body between `Proof.` and `Qed.`

Every ordinary tactic command must end with `.`
A newline does not terminate a tactic command.
Prefer simple one-command-per-line scripts when possible.

Do not output:
- `Proof.`
- `Qed.`
- `Lemma` / `Theorem` declarations
- imports (`Require Import`, …)
- prose, comments, markdown fences, or headings
- any text that is not part of the tactic script
